Notes
  • ↗ Studio tracks only. Search results and Spotify playlists resolve to official YouTube Music releases — no remixes, intros, or visualizers.
  • ↗ Spotify audio is DRM-protected. Tracks are not downloaded from Spotify — only the metadata (title, artist) is used to find the matching release on YouTube Music.
  • ↗ Configurable bitrate floor. Prevents low-bitrate streams (like 48 kbps AAC) from needlessly inflating into oversized files, adapting to your preferences.
  • — Max 200 MB per file.
